A make.fun entry for **Two Lines to Your Past Self** (`u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca`, $2, escrowed).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes two
lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`: a warning to the writer's past
self about one specific moment — signing a car loan at the bank counter
on a Tuesday morning — and one sentence on what that signature cost:
five years of debt for a car parked for good eleven months later.

The output is UTF-8, inside the competition's 1–280 character gate, and
deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical files.

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
# writes output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt when set)
```

See [BUILD-NOTES.md](BUILD-NOTES.md) for the design and local test
results, and [prompt.txt](prompt.txt) for the creative brief.
